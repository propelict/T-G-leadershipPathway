Strategic blocks

Uninterrupted windows, 90 minutes to two hours, for deep thinking, planning, and the work that requires sustained focus.

Schedule these when your energy is highest, and protect them. Treat a meeting scheduled over a strategic block the same way you would treat a meeting scheduled over a client call.

Operational blocks

Shorter windows, 30 to 60 minutes, batched together for decisions, responses, and coordination.

Batching operational work reduces the cost of context-switching and creates cleaner boundaries between modes.

Buffer blocks

Short gaps, 15 to 30 minutes, between different types of work.

They exist to catch overflow, reset mentally, and prevent one task from bleeding into the next.
