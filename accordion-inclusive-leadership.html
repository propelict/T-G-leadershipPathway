Rotate who facilitates

Rotate who facilitates team discussions, rather than always leading from the front yourself.

This builds facilitation skills across the team and shifts the dynamic from "everyone waiting for the founder's view" to genuine collaborative thinking.

Pay attention

Notice whose ideas get picked up in conversation and whose get overlooked.

Research on group dynamics consistently shows that some voices carry more weight than others, often based on factors unrelated to the quality of the idea.

Be explicit

Be explicit about your team's values and norms, not just about what you are building but about how you expect people to treat each other.

Written norms are easier to revisit, update, and hold one another accountable to than implicit ones.
