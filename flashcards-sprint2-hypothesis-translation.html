Sprint 1 finding

Communication

Using SBI for feedback produced more constructive responses in planned one-on-one conversations, but broke down in the moment during team meetings.

Sprint 2 hypothesis

"I believe that preparing a brief SBI note before team meetings where I anticipate giving feedback will help me apply the framework consistently in real-time. I will test this in my next four team meetings and note whether my in-the-moment feedback follows the structure."

Sprint 1 finding

Delegation

Clearer outcome framing at the point of delegation reduced follow-up questions from one team member, but not another.

Sprint 2 hypothesis

"I believe the difference is that one team member needs context on the 'why' behind a task in order to make good decisions independently. I will test adding a brief rationale to my delegation conversations with that person for two weeks and track whether the number of check-ins decreases."

Sprint 1 finding

Accountability

A weekly written check-in improved follow-through on agreed actions, but team members were treating it as a reporting exercise rather than a reflection.

Sprint 2 hypothesis

"I believe that adding one open question to the check-in template ('What's one thing that's getting in your way right now?') will shift it from reporting to dialogue and surface blockers earlier. I will test this over two weeks and note whether the responses change in quality and whether any new issues surface that would otherwise have gone unmentioned."
